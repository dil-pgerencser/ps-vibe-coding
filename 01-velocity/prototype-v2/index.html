How Roster verifies

Government ID: liveness video + document scan matched against state DMV records. Employment: direct outreach to HR contacts at named employers, not self-reported. Portfolio provenance: direct email to credited client contacts with a confirmation link. Business registration: API query against TX Secretary of State database. Insurance: COI reviewed and expiry tracked; we email providers 60 days before lapse. Payout account: micro-deposit verification to confirm account ownership.
